Philosophy

Eternal Philosophy — Human Thought Is Eternal.

We inherit the wisdom of the past, question today’s assumptions, and carry new technology and ideas into the future. From the classics to the cutting edge: whatever the era, people keep thinking. Eternal Philosophy is the operator and holds the “why” — why we think and why we create.

EQUA — Equality, Quality, Alliance

EQUA is a name built on three ideas: Equality, Quality, and Alliance. Grounded in equality, pursuing quality, and moving society forward through alliance. EQUA is the brand that holds the “what” — the kind of society we want to build.

Whatever the size of a business, and whether or not it has IT specialists, everyone should be able to use high-quality tools. We design tools around real work together with the people who use them, and we value long-term collaboration.

The world we aim for: free of discrimination, dialogue over conflict, full of opportunity, and always progressing.

Advancing humanity through equality, quality, and alliance.
