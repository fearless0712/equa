About EQUA

What EQUA is

EQUA is a brand that turns small everyday problems at work and at home into simple tools.

Invoices, tidying up data, checking your phone or your passwords — we take the small, tedious jobs of everyday life and small business and turn them into templates and tools you can use right away.

How we check

Where rules or numbers matter, we check them against official sources and test the tools before publishing. Please check anything important against the official source.
