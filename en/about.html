About EQUA

What EQUA is

EQUA is a brand run by Eternal Philosophy. Our message is “Expanding what your business can do, with technology.” We help businesses get more done through automation and better use of their data.

Invoices, quotes, monthly reports, tidying up data, checking your phone or your passwords — we turn tedious, repetitive jobs into free tools, templates and business tools you can use right away. We also build tools around the way a business already works (custom tools).

How we check

Where rules or numbers matter, we check them against official sources and test the tools before publishing. Please check anything important against the official source.
