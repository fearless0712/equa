Scope

What we do

  • Tools and templates that run in Excel or Google Sheets
  • Business tools that run in a desktop browser (quotes, invoices, records, deadlines)
  • Reworking your existing sheets and documents
  • Clean-up, conversion and totals for CSV and other data
  • Planning how to bring generative AI into your work, internal how-to guides, and automations with AI built in

What we don't take on

  • Large-scale system development or server operation
  • 24-hour maintenance or monitoring
  • On-site work
  • Work that requires logging in to your servers

FAQ

Can I just ask a question first?

Yes. Tell us what you are dealing with and we will tell you whether a tool could help and how we would approach it.

Can you work with Google Sheets?

Yes. We automate Google Sheets with Apps Script as well as Excel.

How is my data handled?

What data we receive, and what happens to it after the work, is agreed with you in advance. Column names only, or a copy with the numbers removed, is fine.

How many rounds of changes are included?

The number and scope of changes are agreed when we quote.

How long does it take?

It depends on what we build, so we tell you when we quote.

Is there a monthly fee?

Only if you want one. Monthly plans are optional after delivery: Light ¥20,000/month (up to 2 changes or consultations) or Standard ¥50,000/month (up to 4 hours of additional development, with priority support).
